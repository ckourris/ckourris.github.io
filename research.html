Research

My work in theoretical physics is focused in the intersection between geometric frustration and Kondo screening in various strongly correlated quantum systems.

In plain English:
The concept of emergence refers to the situation where in a system of a large number of constituents - whatever they may be - there arises collective behaviour of the constituents which would have been virtually impossible to even understand only by focusing on the nature of the elementary constituents. For example, traffic emerges in a system of a road when a very large number of vehicles comes together. What drives emergence, are the ineractions between the constituents. This gives rise to concepts of phase transitions like ice, magnetism and superconductivity.
